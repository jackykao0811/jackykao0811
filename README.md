## 高堯楷 Yao-Kai Kao, MD

TCM physician in Taipei. PhD candidate, Institute of Brain Science, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU).

I work on one question from several directions: **can geometric and spectral structure be used as a quantitative language for biological and physical systems?** Most repositories here are the working code behind that question.

### Research threads

**Geometric pharmacology (Genesis)**
Predicting kinase-inhibitor behaviour from ligand geometry — catechol-bidentate coordination, planarity, and gatekeeper accessibility. In a blinded MAP4K2 assay the framework achieved 5/5 binary accuracy with the correct potency ranking. Predictions for other kinases, including c-Abl, remain **hypotheses pending recombinant kinase assay** and are not presented as validated results.
→ [`luoshu-druggability`](https://github.com/jackykao0811/luoshu-druggability) · Genesis v2 archived at [10.5281/zenodo.20187149](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20187149)

**Spectral network analysis**
Applying the Fiedler value (λ₂) of graph Laplacians to brain imaging, clinical-trial collaboration networks, and industrial clusters. λ₂ is treated strictly as a measure of **network cohesion and integration** — not of competitiveness, causality, leadership, or clinical efficacy.

**Discrete geometry and symmetry constraints**
Symmetry-constrained projection operators and lattice methods, including an E8 projection of hydrogen orbital densities and a symmetry-constrained discrete lattice Boltzmann implementation.
→ [`genesis-e8-orbital-projection`](https://github.com/jackykao0811/genesis-e8-orbital-projection) · [`genesis-3d-hlbm`](https://github.com/jackykao0811/genesis-3d-hlbm) · [`hetu`](https://github.com/jackykao0811/hetu) · [`geometric-physics-luoshu`](https://github.com/jackykao0811/geometric-physics-luoshu)

**State-space compression for search (GSSE / LSP-Go)**
Topological feature embeddings for Go positions — a 21-dimensional representation reaching ~11.6× compression, used to test whether exact topological structure improves life-and-death reasoning.
→ [`lsp-go-engine`](https://github.com/jackykao0811/lsp-go-engine)

**Biomedical signal geometry**
Orbit- and entropy-based descriptors for physiological time series (pulse waveform, HRV, CGM), evaluated against conventional indices rather than in place of them.
→ [`OilPulse-Genesis`](https://github.com/jackykao0811/OilPulse-Genesis)

### A note on scope

Several repositories here explore speculative frameworks — including symmetry-based approaches to open problems in fluid dynamics and gauge theory. **These are exploratory preprints and working code, not claimed solutions.** Where a result has been validated, I state the validation; where it has not, I label it a hypothesis. I would rather be corrected early than cited wrongly.

Issues and corrections are genuinely welcome.
